## Official statements and exact differences

The original `Statements.lean` had three mathematically false degenerate cases. With the user's authorization, those three statements—and only those three—were repaired before proof:

1. `editDist_eq_zero_iff` gained `(hfree : ∃ B : BMat n m, Free H B)`.
   - The forward direction needs the distance infimum to be attained. Without a free comparison matrix, `editDist` is `sInf ∅ = 0`, even though the host may not be free.
   - This extra premise is exactly the existence condition required by the Lean definition; it replaces the paper's implicit nondegeneracy.
2. `editDist_blockDiag_one` gained `(hk : 0 < k)` after `hH`.
   - The paper assumes its pattern is nonempty. Together with `NoZeroLine H`, `hk` supplies `0 < l` and an entry equal to `true`.
3. `editDist_blockDiag` gained the same `(hk : 0 < k)` after `hH`.

`statement-changes.json` compares all 13 original theorem statement texts with their final statement texts. `statement-preservation-final.log` checks 310 original user-level declarations during assembly: none disappears, changes kind, or changes elaborated type/body. `OrderedRemoval/Statements.lean` and the completed attachment have identical SHA-256
`b2625ddfcff3d792375faa0ed3f05f3ee15c7e83b4ec7755825c40438082c5d4`.

The explicit infinite-family declarations are **new results derived from** the direct-sum corollary, not claims that an unchanged official infinite-family statement already existed in the attachment. Infinitude is represented by an injective sequence in the dependent type `Pattern := Σ d : ℕ × ℕ, BMat d.1 d.2`; `(k+t,l+t)` determines `t`.

## Proof notes

- **Step 1** injects each direct-sum copy into a base copy plus extra index maps; that supplies both freeness of the edited host and the counting bound.
- **Step 2** uses an optimal base edit witness and exact Hamming computation on block diagonals.
- **Step 3** uses only finite sums. In the general direct-sum argument, the good-pick inequality is summed over all row/column picks. Bad-row and bad-column costs are counted by fibre multiplicities; bad new-new picks are counted by changed entries. Multiplication and cancellation are performed over positive natural numbers only: no probability, measures, expectation, or division is used.
- **Zero columns** use the same finite-sum style with one newly picked column per group and no `Good` event.
- **Counterexample transfer** uses `ε' = ε/s²`, with `s = 1+pq` for direct sums and `s = 1+q` for zero columns, and verifies `ε' ≤ 1`, distance preservation, and the real-power identity using `Real.div_rpow` and `Real.rpow_mul`.
- The degenerate empty-pattern case has polynomial removal; this is why the existing corollary statements need no extra `hk` hypothesis.
